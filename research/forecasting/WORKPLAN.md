# Coordination and release plan

The seven workstreams have started as software/documentation, not seven completed scientific studies. Leads and individual author responsibilities are unassigned pending the team's agreement. The listed manuscript authors are not automatically assigned field, statistical or institutional responsibilities.

No deadline for validated forecasts is promised. With the current date after the usual monsoon field campaign, a prospective seasonal study needs planning around an upcoming locally appropriate monitoring season. Historical data access and instrument lead times determine the schedule.

## Readiness gates

**Gate A: study ready.** Approve target, sources, inventory/coverage rules, site permissions and lead assignments. Assess whether data support the intended sample size; redesign rather than manufacture negatives if not.

**Gate B: retrospective experiment ready.** Freeze eligible real data and partitions, validate availability timestamps, compare baselines and audit for leakage. The 1,500-row synthetic run does not satisfy this gate.

**Gate C: prospective study ready.** Freeze a reviewed candidate, verify monitoring and logging, define outage handling and verify outcomes independently. Conduct shadow evaluation; never retrospectively edit an issued forecast.

**Gate D: publication/release ready.** Independent reproduction, complete limitations, permissions, author approval and reviewed citation/novelty claim. Public warnings require a separate institutional decision.

## Requests to prepare (not sent)

* GSI/state/district authorities: request dated event polygons, mapping/survey coverage, source uncertainty and research reuse conditions. Ask about existing local forecasting work before choosing a comparison district.
* Rainfall providers/local institutions: request gauge siting, calibration, temporal resolution, historical gaps, issue/publication times and permission to compare with IMERG.
* Field collaborators: identify qualified site lead, access permissions, instrument/calibration support, maintenance responsibility and independent event verification capacity.

No messages or data requests have been sent by this development cycle. Keep associated correspondence in private institutional storage.
