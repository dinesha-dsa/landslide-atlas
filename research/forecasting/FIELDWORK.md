# Fieldwork planning pack — no sites installed

Start with a desk-based appraisal and qualified engineering-geology assessment of accessible candidate slopes. Record landslide mechanism, geology/weathering, drainage, vegetation, road cuts, existing protection, line-of-sight suitability and observation coverage. Select both affected and monitored comparison slopes under a documented sampling strategy. Do not visit unstable slopes during dangerous weather or install equipment without qualified site assessment, institutional/landowner authorization and a field safety plan.

## Proposed measurements

| Measurement | Purpose | Planning and QA requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Local tipping-bucket rain gauge | Compare satellite rainfall with local intensity/accumulation | Record calibration, tip resolution, siting/obstruction, clock drift, maintenance and missing intervals |
| Soil moisture at justified depths | Assess antecedent wetness | Characterize soil-specific calibration, depth, temperature sensitivity and representativeness |
| Piezometer/pore pressure where justified | Observe hydrologic response | Qualified design, installation permissions, depth and datum; sensor-specific calibration |
| Crack/extensometer or GNSS where appropriate | Observe local displacement | Stable reference, accuracy/precision, sampling interval, temperature effects and independent checks |
| Repeat mapped observations/photos | Verify failures, timing and mechanism | Stable unit IDs, standardized survey effort, date/time bounds, positional accuracy and source consent |

Sensors are options, not a purchase specification. Final selection and logging frequency must follow mechanism, expected rate of change, budget and site assessment. No instrument quote, procurement, installed sensor count or guaranteed accuracy is asserted.

## Operating records

Use `templates/site_register.csv`, `sensor_observations.csv`, and `survey_coverage.csv`. Record sensor serial/model, calibration method/date, units, datum/reference, observation time, reception time and quality flags. Preserve raw and corrected values separately. Never replace telemetry loss with zero rainfall/displacement. Track battery, communications, clock synchronization, sensor drift and maintenance. Keep raw high-frequency observations before aggregation. Define detection limits and a minimum detectable change before interpreting deformation.

Keep personally identifying information and unreleased precise site information outside public releases. Field observations require an independent reviewer and documented labelling disagreements. Field installation and any public alert thresholds require the appropriate institutional process; this repository is a research preparation tool.
